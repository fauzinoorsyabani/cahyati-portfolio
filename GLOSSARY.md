# Cahyati Portfolio

Personal portfolio presenting Cahyati's academic record, data engineering internship work, analytical capabilities, certifications, and leadership mandates sourced from her ATS curriculum vitae.

## Language

**Practitioner**:
The portfolio owner (Cahyati), an Information Systems graduate specializing in data engineering, ETL workflows, and business intelligence.
_Avoid_: User, author, photographer, freelancer

**Capability**:
A core technical or operational discipline offered by the Practitioner, showcased in the interactive index (for example, ETL & Data Pipelines or BI & Visualization).
_Avoid_: Service, offering, skill item

**Case Study**:
A concrete field project, internship deliverable, or analytical artifact executed by the Practitioner, including scope, tools, and measurable outcome.
_Avoid_: Work item, photo shoot, portfolio card

**Credential**:
A verified formal qualification, certification, institutional affiliation, or competition award earned by the Practitioner (such as BNSP Data Analyst certification, RevoU Tech Academy completion, or Olympiad medals).
_Avoid_: Badge, logo, certificate item

**Leadership Mandate**:
A formal organizational office or committee responsibility held by the Practitioner across university student bodies and events.
_Avoid_: Club activity, volunteering, side role

**Track Record Highlight**:
A verified impact summary pairing measurable evidence from the Practitioner's CV with a representative editorial visual, presented without fabricated third-party quotes.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, review, client quote

**Metric Highlight**:
A headline quantitative proof point drawn directly from the Practitioner's CV (such as `1,000+` inventoried airport GSE units, `3.92` GPA, or `7+` certifications and medals).
_Avoid_: Stat box, KPI widget, bento tile

**Gallery Frame**:
A curated monochrome editorial photograph displayed inside the interactive 3D showcase cylinder or visual strips.
_Avoid_: Slide, photo tile, media item

**Inquiry**:
A recruitment, collaboration, or project contact message composed by a visitor and dispatched via the Practitioner's direct email channel.
_Avoid_: Booking request, lead, form payload

**FAQ Entry**:
A paired recruiter question and verified answer addressing role availability, technical stack, internship scope, certifications, or relocation readiness.
_Avoid_: Accordion item, help entry
